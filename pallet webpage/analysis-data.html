TRACEABILITY

Source files & definitions

Downloads are local snapshots copied with the webpage. The source path and SHA-256 fingerprint identify the exact file used. Rebuild the webpage dataset to refresh these snapshots after source analyses change.

  • Observed mean: arithmetic mean of available workbook trial values for a box and hour.
  • First observed trial ≥ 4: earliest recorded hour when any individual trial reaches the threshold. It is not the first mean crossing.
  • Simulated mean: arithmetic mean of 500 saved samples at the box-hour. Samples were centered on observed averages, so close agreement is not independent validation.
  • Units: the cleaned analysis uses “value”; its measurement unit and the operational meaning of threshold 4 remain unconfirmed. Exceedance is not a validated spoilage probability.
  • Exposed surfaces: source metadata. Inferred outer sides are a geometry check only; layer separation and drawing gaps do not change the real exposure count.
